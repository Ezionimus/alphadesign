### Title
**AI-Powered De Novo Protein Design Pipeline for Longevity Therapeutics — Zero-Capital, Open-Source Computational Discovery**

### Problem
Longevity research needs novel proteins (enzymes, binders, therapeutics) but experimental screening costs millions. Computational design can short-circuit this by generating 100+ candidate sequences in hours — but most tools require expensive GPUs or cloud compute.

### Solution
We built a **zero-capital, GPU-accelerated protein design pipeline** using:
- ESM-2 (650M & 35M) masked language models
- CORRECTED pseudo-perplexity scoring (masked LM, not left-to-right)
- Iterative masked refinement for sequence generation
- Automated contest discovery + IP tracking + submission packaging
